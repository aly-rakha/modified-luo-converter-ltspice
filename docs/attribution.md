# Sources and attribution

This is a student replication and analysis by **Aly Hany Rakha**, originally developed for CO-526 Electronics Laboratory in October 2026.

The converter topology and theoretical starting point are from H. Gholizadeh, M. Dehghan, G. B. Gharehpetian and A. Yazdani, “Triple, Quadruple, and Hextuple Voltage-Lift Modified Luo Converters Suitable for Renewable Applications,” IECON 2024. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1109/IECON55916.2024.10905089.

The topology image in the report is attributed to that paper and is excluded from any project licensing grant. The paper itself is not included. All waveform figures are actual LTspice captures from the project; cropping removes surrounding interface without redrawing traces. LTspice and the IPP600N25N3 model are third-party dependencies; their libraries are not redistributed.

The repository packages simulations, analysis, and documentation developed with AI assistance. The reported values come from the archived LTspice logs; the project does not claim original invention of the converter topology or hardware validation.
